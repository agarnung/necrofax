# Product steering

## Vision

NecroFax is a fax machine for the other side. It revives a dead technology
with a real frame format, a simplified handshake and a thermal-paper fax. The
machine is the interface; the paper is the content.

## Audience

People who remember fax machines, people who like ghost stories and interactive
fiction, and judges looking for a resurrection rather than a themed dashboard.

## Principles

- **Dead tech, alive.** A real codec and a real handshake, not a skin.
- **The paper is the product.** The fax is readable, printable and physical.
- **One screen, one machine.** Receive, read, reply, send and archive.
- **Local and private.** No network, no accounts, no tracking, no real data.
- **Accessible by default.** Native controls, live status, reduced motion.
- **Small and finished.** 5 faxes, 5 ghosts, 34 tests, no build step.

## Category

**Resurrection** — the fax machine is the dead technology; the app is the revival.
